Read the Header Before the Value

Four Parts of a Field, Read Separately

A value only means something inside its field. Read the label, the value, the row context, and the unknown — in that order, without letting them blur together.

Part 01 — Field label

What the header may express

Read the column header first. It names what the cell is supposed to carry — not what you hope it carries.

Part 02 — Visible value

What the cell actually shows

Record exactly what is displayed in the cell. If nothing is displayed, that absence is the visible value.

Part 03 — Row context

What the neighbors provide

Use the header, the adjacent cells, and the source position to understand how this row should be read.

Part 04 — Unknown meaning

What still cannot be determined

Mark whatever remains unclear instead of guessing. An unknown stays open until the page resolves it.

Blank Is Not Zero

A Blank Cell Shows Nothing

A blank, a dash, or a missing value means the cell shows nothing. It must not be read as no, zero, none, or not applicable.

Not shown

An empty cell is an absence of information, not a value. Keep it marked as not shown or unknown until the source page says otherwise.

A blank means

  • The cell currently shows nothing.
  • The value is missing or not displayed.
  • Something to mark as unknown, not to fill in.

A blank does not mean

  • No, zero, or none.
  • Not applicable or not included.
  • A value you are free to invent.

Same Shape, Different Meaning

Re-Confirm Before You Compare Rows

Similar numbers, short labels, and icons can use different units, options, or contexts. Re-confirm the header, unit, format, and source before comparing across rows.

✓Two rows line up when

  • The header is the same for both rows.
  • The unit and format are confirmed equal.
  • The source context matches for both.

✕Do not compare when

  • Different units hide behind the same shape.
  • Different options share a short label.
  • The source context differs between rows.
Field ruleA number, label, or icon is not portable. It carries the unit, option, and context of its own row, and must be re-confirmed before any cross-row comparison.

Do Not Fill the Missing Cell

Know When to Leave It Blank

  • 1

    The header was skipped. You interpreted a value without reading its label. Stop and read the header first.

  • 2

    A blank became a value. You read a blank as no, zero, or not applicable. Stop and restore it to not shown.

  • 3

    Context was ignored. The value was read without its row, source, or neighbors. Stop and re-read in context.

  • 4

    Units were assumed equal. Similar numbers were compared across rows without confirming units. Stop and re-confirm.

  • 5

    An unknown was filled in. You invented a meaning for an open cell. Stop and mark it unknown instead.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a FishGoo spreadsheet?

A FishGoo spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to research, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official FishGoo service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by FishGoo or Google.

What is Field Context reading?

Field Context reading separates four things: the field label (what the column header may express), the visible value (what the cell actually shows), the row context (what the header, neighbors, and source provide), and the unknown meaning (what still cannot be determined).

Does this site provide a data dictionary or verify data?

No. This resource is not a data dictionary service, a product database, a store, a QC certification, a shopping agent, an order tracker, or a fee calculator. It explains how to read, not what the data means.

How do I read a blank cell?

A blank, dash, or missing cell shows nothing, so mark it as not shown or unknown. It must not be automatically read as no, zero, none, or not applicable. A blank is an absence of information, not a value.

Where do I continue after reading a row?

After you have read the field label, visible value, and row context, and re-checked the destination page, you can continue through an agent shopping service to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
